# Sequencing Pipelines

This document describes the standard sequencing pipelines available at NGI.

## Whole Genome Sequencing

The WGS pipeline is used for whole genome sequencing of human samples at 30x coverage.

- **Input**: Raw `.fastq.gz` files from sequencer
- **Output**: VCF, BCF, and summary statistics
- **Typical runtime**: 12-24 hours

## RNA-Seq

The RNA-Seq pipeline handles both standard and single-cell RNA sequencing data.

- **Input**: Raw `.fastq.gz` files
- **Output**: Gene expression matrices, differential expression results
- **Typical runtime**: 8-16 hours
